Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026
This document was drafted with AI assistance as a starting point, not by an attorney. If you operate in — or start serving customers in — a state with its own specific data privacy law (California's CCPA is the most well-known example), it's worth having this reviewed against those specific requirements, since they can call for particular disclosures or rights this draft doesn't attempt to cover.

1. What This Policy Covers

This policy explains what information we collect through Black Hat Community's cards and digital contact pages, how it's used, and who else might see it. It applies to two different groups of people, covered separately below: cardholders (people who own a Card), and visitors (people who tap or scan a Card and choose to share their own information back).

2. Information We Collect From Cardholders

When you order a Card, you provide us the information you want displayed on your own digital page — which may include your name, phone number, email address, mailing address, job title, business name, photos, social media handles, payment handles (like a Cash App or Venmo username), and any other details you choose to include.

This information is displayed publicly on your card's page to anyone who taps or visits it, unless you've asked us to omit a specific field.

Important — third-party visitor information: Some cards include a feature that lets someone visiting your page share their own contact information back to you (for example, so you can follow up with them later). If you use this feature, you are collecting that visitor's personal information yourself — their name, phone number, email, and anything else they choose to enter. We provide the tool that makes this possible, but you, as the cardholder, are the one collecting and receiving that information, and you're responsible for using it appropriately and for telling visitors, if asked, how you intend to use it.

3. How We Use Cardholder Information

  • To build, host, and maintain your Card's digital page
  • To contact you about your order, your Card, or requested updates
  • To generate a QR code and printable materials tied to your Card

We don't sell your information to third parties, and we don't use it for advertising.

4. Where Information Is Stored

Cardholder information is stored as data files in a private code repository and published to a public website through GitHub and Vercel, two third-party hosting providers. This is a straightforward file-based system rather than a traditional database — worth knowing plainly, since it shapes what "deleting" your information actually means in practice: removing your Card also removes the underlying file, but you should know it may persist in that repository's revision history unless separately purged.

Photos and images you send us that are not added to your micro-site are deleted from our systems and email within 30 days of completing your card or micro-site, and are not retained by Adette and Bo. Images that are added to your micro-site are kept as described above.

5. Payment Information

We don't collect or store credit card numbers or bank account details. Payments are made directly through third-party apps (Cash App, Zelle, Venmo, PayPal) according to their own privacy practices, which we don't control. We may retain a record that a payment was made and its amount, for our own bookkeeping.

6. Cookies & Analytics

Our site may use basic, privacy-respecting analytics (such as Vercel's built-in page-view analytics) to understand how many people are visiting cards, without tracking individuals across other websites. We also keep a simple count of how many times each Card is opened, recorded as daily totals (for example, "12 opens on a given day"). These counts do not include your name, IP address, device details, or any other information that identifies you, and they are not linked to anything you share with a cardholder. We may share a Card's own totals with the cardholder it belongs to.

7. Your Choices

  • You can request a correction, update, or removal of your own Card's information at any time — every Card includes a Contact/Support/Updates link for this purpose.
  • If you're a visitor who shared your information with a cardholder and want it removed, that request should go to the cardholder directly, since they're the one who received and holds it — though you're welcome to reach out to us as well if you're unable to reach them.

8. Children's Privacy

This service isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a Card contains information about a child that shouldn't be there, please contact us and we'll address it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the service grows. Meaningful changes will be reflected here with an updated date at the top of this page.
