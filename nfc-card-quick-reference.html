Programming Your First NFC Card

From blank tag to a working "Add to Contacts" tap — start to finish

App: NFC Tools by WAKDEV (free, Android & iOS)
Setup
1

Download NFC Tools

Install from Google Play or the App Store. No account needed.

2

Turn on NFC

Settings → Connected devices → NFC. On by default on most phones — worth a quick check.

Write the card
3

Open Write tab

Launch the app, tap "Write" at the bottom or top of the screen.

4

Add a vCard record

"Add a record" → "Contact (vCard)." Fill in name, phone, email — leave optional fields blank if unused.

5

Confirm & tap Write

Add the record to the queue, then tap the "Write" button to begin.

6

Hold tag to phone

Rest the tag near your phone's camera/top-back for 1–2 seconds until it confirms success.

Verify
7

Read it back

Switch to "Read," tap the tag again, confirm every field looks right.

8

Test the real tap

Close the app, tap the tag like a normal user. Confirm it opens "Add to Contacts" directly.

🔒 Before you lock a tag

  • Don't lock until you've verified the write — locking is permanent
  • Test on both iPhone and Android if the batch will go to a mix of people
  • Keep a couple of spare blank tags for redo attempts

⚠ If the write or tap fails

  • Check NFC is enabled — most common cause
  • Remove phone case — some block the signal
  • Hold steady for 2+ seconds, don't move too soon
  • If it opens a browser instead of Contacts, the vCard formatting may be off — recheck via the Read tab
