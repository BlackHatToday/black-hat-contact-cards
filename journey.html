From Scanned Ad to Opened Letter

The full current path — three early gaps identified and resolved, marked in green

1

Sees the advertisement

A flyer with a QR code — "Tap to share your contact information. $25 Personal / $50 Business — Unlimited Uses." Minimal context by design; the whole pitch is the tagline and the price.

ad-flyer.html / ad-flyer-bracelet.html
2

Scans it, sees what this actually is first

A short intro sits above the form now — what a Black Hat Card is, what happens next, and a link to a quick demo — before asking for a single field of personal info.

my-contact-info-form.html
Resolved — context now precedes data collection, for cold traffic and referred friends alike
3

Fills it in, chooses Text or Email

Two real send options now, not one. If mailto: fails to open anything on their phone, Text is right there as a genuine second path, not a dead end.

Resolved — a real fallback exists if the first method doesn't work
4

Sees "You're All Set!" immediately

A real confirmation screen replaces the form the instant they send — what happens next, a concrete timeline, and a visible way to try the other send option if nothing opened.

Resolved — immediate reassurance no longer depends on you checking your inbox
5

Same fix, same form — for realtors too

property-intake-form.html got the identical Text/Email choice and confirmation panel, minus the cold-traffic intro (realtors aren't usually strangers off a flyer).

property-intake-form.html
6

You reply

Confirms receipt, asks for their mailing address, sets expectations — 7-10 days, payment isn't due until they've tried it.

You build their card

Retyped from their email — your one deliberate review checkpoint. Brand applied if relevant, folder created, pushed live.

You write the physical tag, mail it

Card or bracelet, plus the printed Welcome letter — test steps, the animated demo QR, payment options.

They open the letter

First physical touchpoint with the actual product. The journey mapped here ends the moment this happens.
