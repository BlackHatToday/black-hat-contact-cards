Our Services

At Child & Family Mental Wellness, we believe in the power of therapy to transform lives. Our therapeutic services are designed to provide a safe and supportive environment. Where individuals and families can explore their feelings, beliefs, address challenges, and achieve personal goals.

Individual Therapy

This service provides a focus on breaking unhealthy generational cycles/patterns, showing up emotionally regulated in relationships, and building support systems.

Adolescent (12-19)

This service provides individual therapy with a focus on increasing self awareness, communication, emotional intelligence & regulation, while developing healthy coping skills.

Family Therapy

This service supports family members improving communication while navigating relational stress caused by mental health, blending families, parenting challenges, role confusion, conflict or transitions.

Training/Workshops

I provide workshops and trainings for schools, community organizations, and professionals focused on supporting children, strengthening family relationships.
