Bulk Generator

Turn a CSV of names into finished folders — index.html and a valid data.json for each — written directly into your project. New folders only; anything that already exists gets skipped, never overwritten.

Step 1 — Get the Template
1
Download the CSV template, fill it in, send it to whoever's collecting the names
Columns: FirstName, LastName, Title, Organization, Phone, Email, BrandSlug (BrandSlug is optional — leave blank for the default colors).
Step 2 — Load the Filled-In CSV
2
Choose the completed CSV file
Step 3 — Create the Folders
3
Pick your project's root folder (the one containing person-template/, assets/, etc.)
Requires Chrome or Edge. You'll be asked to pick your project folder, then confirm write access.
