Tap the Card

Hold their Black Hat Card to the back of your phone — just like tapping to pay.

2

Their Card Opens

Their photo, name, and contact info load instantly — right in your browser, no app needed.

3

Save to Contacts

Quickest option — saves their name, phone, and email straight into your phone.

or
3

Save as PDF

Keeps everything — skills, payment info, and anything else that doesn't fit a contact card.

4

Done

They're saved for good — no typing, no missed digits, no lost paper card.

Save to Contacts only captures the basics — a contact card format simply can't hold everything. Save as PDF instead if you want the complete picture.

Want your own? Ask anyone with a Black Hat Card to tap "Refer a Friend" — or just scan the QR on any flyer to request one yourself.
